FAQ

Good questions

Should I repair or replace?

Noise and uneven movement usually mean a repair. Severe or repeated damage often means replacement is the better value. We'll tell you honestly.

How much does a repair cost?

It depends on the issue, the parts and the type of repair. Quotes are always free.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes, 24/7, including weekends and holidays.

Do you work on homes and businesses?

Yes. We work on residential and commercial garage doors, locks, doors and windows across the GTA and surrounding areas.

How far do you travel?

We serve Toronto and the whole GTA, plus surrounding areas up to about 200 km away, including Hamilton, Niagara, Kitchener-Waterloo, Barrie and Orillia. Not sure if you’re in range? Give us a call.

How often should a garage door be serviced?

At least once a year to keep it safe, quiet and long-lasting.
