Good to know

Door questions

Can my door be fixed, or does it need replacing?

Most sticking, latching and draft problems can be fixed with adjustment, new hardware or weatherstripping. If the door or frame is badly damaged, we’ll tell you honestly whether replacement makes more sense.

Do you work on commercial doors?

Yes. We handle residential and commercial doors, including door closers for business entrances.

My door is drafty. Is that an easy fix?

Often, yes. Worn weatherstripping or a door that’s slightly out of alignment are common causes, and both are usually quick to fix.
