Good to know

Locksmith questions

What’s the difference between rekeying and replacing?

Rekeying changes the inside of your existing lock so old keys stop working and new keys do. Replacing swaps the whole lock, which is a good choice if it’s damaged, worn out, or you want an upgrade.

Do I need to rekey after moving into a new home?

It’s strongly recommended. Previous owners, tenants or contractors may still have keys.

What do I need for a lockout?

Photo ID that matches the address, or other proof that you live there or are authorized to access the property.
