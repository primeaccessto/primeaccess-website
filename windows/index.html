Window questions

Can a drafty window be fixed without replacing it?

Often, yes. New weatherstripping, sealing or an adjustment can make a big difference. If the frame or unit is failing, we’ll explain your replacement options.

Do you replace window hardware?

Yes. We repair and replace handles, cranks, hinges and locks for most common window types.

Do you work on business windows too?

Yes, we serve both residential and commercial properties across the GTA.
